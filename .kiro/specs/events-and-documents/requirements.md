# Events and Documents story requirements

What earnings figures does FMP report for this company, and which filings, transcript or news items can I inspect?

This App uses the accepted Batch B earnings calendar, SEC filing metadata, earnings-call transcript and company news. It presents the evidence in that order and ends with a source-bounded conclusion. The App displays provider observations and carries company and query configuration through normal Workspace save and reopen. Calculated surprises, models, new provider coverage and DCF are outside this delivery. [Story brief](brief.md) · [Batch B design](../market-data-integrations/fmp-expansion-b-design.md)

## 1. Select the company and query bounds

1. When an analyst opens Events and Documents from the catalog, the App shall display the story question, the single selected company and its query symbol, and the effective bounds for each of the four inputs.
2. When the analyst changes the company, the App shall apply that symbol to each company-scoped query and to the earnings calendar issuer filter. When the analyst edits the bounds for one input, the App shall apply them to that input and preserve the other inputs' selections.
3. If a choice fails the corresponding Batch B query contract, the App shall identify the invalid field and applicable bound before dispatching the affected request.
4. While evidence for a changed company or query is pending, the App shall clear the superseded evidence and its conclusion contribution, identify the pending input and ignore late results for superseded selections.

## 2. Inspect reported earnings figures

1. When the analyst requests earnings figures, the App shall use one market-wide FMP calendar window with inclusive start and end dates spanning at most seven calendar days. It shall expose the effective window, the returned market-wide row count, the 250-row source-order cap and observed clipping status, then display every returned row matching the selected issuer in source order.
2. When the calendar supplies multiple matching rows, the App shall keep them separately inspectable, including rows with the same report date. Every row shall retain its supplied report date and last update and expose its own source values.
3. When displaying a row, the App shall label estimated and actual EPS and revenue separately. EPS shall be labeled per share and revenue as a monetary amount. The App shall preserve supplied currency and scale, mark unavailable currency and scale as unknown, and label any display scaling so that the source value can be read from the drill-down. Zero shall remain distinct from an unreported amount.
4. When confirmation status, fiscal period or source timezone is unavailable, the App shall mark it unknown wherever it describes the corresponding field. An earnings calendar date shall be labeled as a report date.
5. If the calendar has no returned rows, the App shall identify an empty calendar result. If the calendar returns rows and none match the issuer, the App shall identify the empty issuer filter result. In both cases, coverage beyond the returned market-wide slice shall remain unknown. If rows were clipped, the App shall disclose clipping even when a matching row was found.

6. When figures are available for an earnings row, the App shall pair the row with a chart for each available measure, keeping EPS and revenue on separate scales. Each mark shall identify its row, field and exact source value. An absent figure shall have an explicit missing label and no numeric mark. Duplicate report dates shall remain distinguishable.

## 3. Inspect independently queried documents

1. When requesting SEC filing metadata, the App shall use the selected symbol, an inclusive date window of at most 365 days, page 0–99 and limit 1–100. It shall present the returned filing date, acceptance timestamp at supplied precision, form type, string CIK and supplied document and index links separately. Inline coverage shall be labeled metadata only.
2. When requesting an earnings-call transcript, the App shall use the selected symbol and explicitly selected year and quarter, within 1900–2100 and quarters 1–4. It shall display the exact-period result, supplied timestamp and verbatim text including whitespace. Completeness shall remain unknown unless FMP establishes it. If the response is mismatched, duplicated or lacks required text, the App shall show a validation failure. If the result is empty, the App shall label that period unavailable and retain the selected period.
3. When requesting company news, the App shall use the selected symbol, page 0–99, limit 1–50 and either no dates or an inclusive date pair spanning at most 31 days. It shall show the supplied publication timestamp, site, publisher and article link. The supplied text shall be labeled a partial excerpt. A headline without text shall be labeled metadata only.
4. When an analyst opens a document result, the App shall identify the issuer, source, source date or period, effective query and bounds, retrieval time and content status. The effective date, pagination and period selectors shall remain independent of any selected earnings row. Any cross-reference that groups items in the story shall convey shared company context only.
5. When the App displays provider text or links, it shall render the text literally and offer only validated HTTP(S) link targets. If a link is absent or invalid, the App shall preserve the valid metadata and omit the link action. Remote embedded content and link prefetch shall be absent. When a source serves a full page, the App shall retain the stated limit and shall leave additional coverage unknown.

## 4. Read the conclusion and recover

1. When each input resolves, the App shall summarize the matching calendar evidence and the separately available filing, transcript and news evidence. Every reported amount shall be traceable to a matching row and field, and every reported document availability claim shall be traceable to a returned item or result with the displayed content status.
2. When usable current evidence is available for all four inputs, the App shall label the conclusion **Evidence available** and identify any missing numeric fields and partial, metadata-only or uncertain content. For this conclusion an earnings row is usable if it contains at least one supplied actual or estimated figure. A valid current metadata item counts for filings or news. A transcript counts when it contains valid text for the selected issuer and fiscal period. Stale items do not contribute to any conclusion.
3. When one, two or three inputs supply usable current evidence, the App shall label the conclusion **Partial evidence** and identify which input and condition limit the answer. When no input supplies usable current evidence and any input is pending, the App shall show that the conclusion is pending. When no input supplies usable current evidence and all inputs have resolved, the App shall label the conclusion **Insufficient evidence** and distinguish empty results, missing figures and failures. Each state applies only to the effective queries and their returned slices.
4. When browsing an evidence step, the analyst shall see FMP named in the headline or subtitle and shall be able to inspect source rows, dates, retrieval time, effective bounds, freshness and any acquisition or validation issue affecting the step.
5. If acquisition or validation fails, the App shall show the available safe typed reason, retain the current choices and preserve independently valid steps. Previously acquired evidence for the same query may remain visible with a stale label and its original retrieval time. When the analyst retries successfully, the App shall replace the failed or stale evidence and recompute the conclusion for the current selections.

## 5. Use, save and verify the App

1. When using either Workspace theme with a keyboard, the analyst shall be able to select a company, edit all query bounds, inspect each row or document, return focus to its invoking control and reach the conclusion.
2. When the analyst saves the App or exports an app template, the saved or exported configuration shall contain the story and widget layout and selected query and chart display choices. Provider rows, figures, excerpts, transcript text, response-derived links, computed conclusions and credentials shall be absent. When the analyst reopens the App in a fresh session, it shall restore the choices and reacquire the data through the normal installed routes.
3. When verifying release readiness, the evidence record shall identify the candidate builds, browser environment, query tuples, observed coverage and provider request counts. An isolated browser journey using real FMP responses shall cover catalog launch, visible facts for all four inputs, including chart marks and missing values checked against their own source fields in the corresponding acquired responses, issuer and applicable query changes, save, fresh-session reopen and safe export. If entitlement prevents an input check, live acceptance for that input shall remain open. [Testing guidance](../../steering/testing.md)
4. When verifying edge cases, the evidence record shall identify controlled cases separately from real FMP acceptance. Controlled coverage shall exercise multiple matching rows, zero and missing earnings, clipped and empty calendars, absent and partial content, mismatched or superseded evidence, typed failure and recovery, links and literal text, keyboard navigation and both themes. Verification artifacts shall omit credentials and raw licensed content.
