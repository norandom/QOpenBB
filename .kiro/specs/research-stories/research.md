# Research story integration findings

Discovery on 26 November 2026 used QOpenBB revision `6fee042` and its Workspace integration baseline. The catalog, authenticated bridge, verified factor methods and saved-run store provide the delivery path. Live acquisition of model-ready point-in-time inputs is the first open dependency. The Kiro design workflow and repository steering guided this integration review.

| Question | Source finding | Design consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Where should the App enter Workspace? | The catalog adds curated layouts after adapting imported Apps. Workspace hydration reads chart models and chart views. [Catalog generation](../../../pipeline/qopenbb_ci/catalog.py) | Generate one curated declaration, hydrate approved chart settings and keep the current shell and widget dispatcher. |
| What survives a save? | Workspace can restore an issuer run by reference. Dashboard sync and app-template export take separate paths. [Widget acceptance](../../../artifacts/reviewed/factor-signals/workspace-modeling-widgets.md) | Use a shared allowlist for persisted story state and keep source rows in transient memory. Test both serialized artifacts. |
| Can one run validate all three steps? | The pair descriptor returns both price metrics and their spreads. DFII10 has its own descriptor, independent checks and run. [Comparison](../../../services/data-api/qopenbb_data/modeling/models/factors/comparison.py) · [Real yield](../../../services/data-api/qopenbb_data/modeling/models/factors/real_yield.py) | Use two serial evaluations at the same cutoff. Bind each visible result to its individual verification evidence. Use exact wire decimals for the verdict. |
| Can an output widget supply all evidence? | The projection includes trace and verification. The authorized run includes the input snapshot containing the cited rows. [Projection](../../../services/data-api/qopenbb_data/modeling/service/widget_projection.py) · [Run contract](../../../services/data-api/qopenbb_data/modeling/contracts/results.py) | Join source IDs to rows from the saved run. Rereading a provider could substitute a subsequent revision. |
| Do existing real inputs establish historical availability? | The ETF checkpoint covers two disjoint windows and one technology ETF. The current acquisition adapter documents the lack of verified historical vintage timestamps in its ordinary FRED response. [Coverage](../../../docs/provider-coverage.md) · [Acquisition adapter](../../../services/data-api/qopenbb_data/modeling/service/acquisition.py) | Require timestamped source evidence for each accepted window and qualify an alternate technology ETF with its actual listing calendar. A successful price request alone cannot close this gate. |
| What defines an expected session? | The exchange publishes NYSE Arca trading hours and closure schedules. The current schedule page alone cannot establish the revision available on an arbitrary historical date. [NYSE calendar](https://www.nyse.com/trade/hours-calendars) | Pin a dated exchange schedule artifact with its coverage, effective revisions and source digest. Reject uncovered dates. Preserve holidays, special closures and partial trading days. |

The story-specific controller keeps one selection and two authorized references. This avoids separate widgets racing to publish a verdict and avoids adding a general workflow engine for a single story. Existing owner isolation and saved-run reads remain the authority on access. The published browser evidence covers the issuer journey. Real-input acceptance of the new story is pending.
