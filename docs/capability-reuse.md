# Build the next research App on existing OpenBB capabilities

Use the existing App, widget parameters, linked groups and renderers for the next Company Research delivery. First prove that one named company can drive its research views and survive save/reopen; then expand provider coverage. A second dashboard or selector framework is justified only if an observed requirement cannot be met by the existing implementation.

This 2026-10-28 inventory separates shipped evidence from source availability. Workspace source links are pinned to `22180d8ec206e088a384f2a3c7345358b1832c7c`. The [market-data](../.kiro/specs/market-data-integrations/requirements.md) and [analyst-workspace](../.kiro/specs/analyst-workspace/requirements.md) requirements are scope stubs, not approved designs. The sequence below proposes their first bounded slice; it does not authorize all later implementation.

## Reuse before extending

**Accepted/runtime bounded** means an identified application journey passed. **Source present/configuration needed** means the mechanism exists but the proposed App is not accepted. **Unverified** means source alone cannot establish the required behavior. **Missing** identifies an absent installed configuration or catalog entry, without claiming the upstream API lacks it.

## Deliver one Company Research App first

After the current delivery, uv/Python packaging and Valkey work, specify a Company Research App around a shared name/symbol selection, with price, company profile, statements, news, earnings calls and analyst views. Begin with the existing profile/income widgets and group mechanism; add the absent views in small accepted increments. Keep unavailable views explicit until their installed catalog and provider paths work. Do not present uploaded historical gallery data as live research coverage.

Acceptance of each increment requires four connected proofs:

1. **Source:** identify the reused mechanism and any necessary production delta, under one owning spec and criterion.
2. **Configuration:** exercise the installed catalog/App, parameter defaults, dependent options and shared group membership in the real application.
3. **Real provider:** compare returned values or content with a reference and label symbol identity, dates, currency, units, period/frequency, provenance and entitlement. Handle missing data and provider failure visibly.
4. **Save/reopen:** change the company and relevant period, save, reopen in a fresh authenticated context and recreate the disposable container with retained state. Verify the selected instrument, layout, widget parameters and group membership, alongside clean diagnostics.

Then expand FMP research families and add yfinance/FRED or later providers for explicit gaps. A common control does not make their instruments, adjustment rules or frequencies interchangeable. Record actual tests and evidence in the [breadth map](../.kiro/breadth.toml); planned checks remain planned. Reconsider reuse only when a reproducible failure shows that the existing mechanism cannot meet an accepted requirement.

Scope groups by research meaning. A Company group can drive FMP profile, statements, news and estimates. A Date Range group can align equity, commodity and macro views without replacing a FRED series identifier with an equity symbol. Provider-specific instrument resolution and fiscal-period alignment remain data contracts to verify; existing parameter propagation does not solve them automatically.
