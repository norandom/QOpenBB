# Brief: research-stories

## Problem

Quants and portfolio managers judge a research tool by whether its reasoning can be followed and checked. QOpenBB's shipped Apps are collections of tables: the starter App lists 30 widgets on one tab, and Company Research groups statements without leading anywhere. Nothing takes an analyst from a question, through evidence, to a conclusion.

## Current State

- Curated Apps are generated by the catalog pipeline; only Company Research and the chart gallery have builders.
- One shared company selection can drive several widgets through parameter groups. A shared date range is required but not implemented.
- 36 chart configurations are accepted, but a curated App cannot ship a saved chart on a live widget: the catalog drops that state.
- Batch B widgets (events and documents) exist only in the starter App.

## Desired Outcome

Story Apps shipped in the catalog. Each story:

1. opens with one stated question
2. presents ordered steps, each pairing a claim with a chart and a drill-down to its source rows, provenance and check states
3. shows counter-evidence and data limits
4. ends in a stated conclusion: supported, not supported or insufficient data

Changing the subject (an asset pair, a sector pair or a company) re-runs every step. No step keeps data from the previous subject, and missing data is explained inline.

The first story is "Commodities vs tech: which regime are we in?". Later stories cover events and documents, a DCF drill-down with gated validation steps, and the upgraded Company Research App.

## Approach

Add a story builder to the catalog pipeline that composes existing widgets, charts and modeling-service result widgets into ordered steps. Reuse the existing Workspace shell, controls and themes. Apply terminal-grade polish (dense linked layout, keyboard access, consistent terminology, visible provenance and freshness) to the first story before repeating the pattern.

## Scope

- **In**:
  - a story definition format and catalog builder
  - shared subject and date context across all steps
  - saved chart views on live and result widgets within a story
  - step states and the conclusion state, driven by check results
  - the first story, then Events and Documents, DCF drill-down and Company Research stories
  - real-data browser acceptance per story: initial load, subject change, save, reopen, export, failure and recovery
- **Out**:
  - computing any metric (factor-signals and financial-modeling)
  - new provider adapters
  - a new shell, toolbar or visual identity
  - generated narrative text that states conclusions the checks do not support

## Boundary Candidates

- story definition and catalog generation
- shared subject and date context
- step and conclusion state presentation
- individual stories, one per delivery

## Out of Boundary

- formulas, thresholds and regime methodology
- provider licensing decisions for an installation
- AI-written commentary

## Upstream / Downstream

- **Upstream**: factor-signals (results and check states); openbb-self-service (shared date context, saved chart state in curated Apps); market-data-integrations (verified inputs, Batch B and Batch C); analyst-workspace (Company Research App)
- **Downstream**: private-signal-seam (stories that show externally supplied signals); later AI and Excel consumers

## Existing Spec Touchpoints

- **Extends**: none
- **Adjacent**: analyst-workspace (Company Research, chart renderers); openbb-self-service (catalog generation, parameter groups)

## Constraints

- Preserve the OpenBB experience: no redesign, no hidden or disabled features (`steering/workspace-experience.md`).
- Every chart step is accepted with real provider data through the application; entitlement failures stay visible.
- FRED-based steps carry the FRED notice and name the series source in the headline.
- Saved stories and exports hold choices and configuration only, never response text.
- 400/300/50 complexity caps for any production code.

## Source attribution

Every chart and widget names its data source in the headline or subtitle, for example "Source: FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis". The source institution for each verified input is in `docs/reference/cross-asset-inputs.json`. Owner decision of 2026-11-18: this label is the whole attribution rule; FRED series are not excluded over rights labels.
