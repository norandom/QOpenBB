# Events and Documents story brief

What earnings figures does FMP report for this company, and which filings, transcript or news items can I inspect?

The next curated Workspace App will answer this question for one selected company. It will show the FMP earnings calendar rows returned for the selected window, visually distinguish reported amounts from estimates for each available measure, then let the analyst inspect independently queried SEC filing metadata, an earnings-call transcript for the selected year and quarter, and company news. The conclusion will cite the available evidence and identify inputs with empty results, missing fields or acquisition failures.

Batch B supplies all four inputs. Its market-wide earnings calendar can be clipped before issuer filtering, and a bounded lookup cannot establish complete coverage. A filing or article near the calendar report date does not establish a link to an earnings event. The transcript period comes from its own query. The App will preserve separate query bounds and show their source, dates, retrieval time and limits at the point of inspection. [Batch B provider coverage](../../../docs/provider-coverage.md)

The story reuses the catalog, Workspace controls and evidence drill-down from the research-story workflow. Saved dashboards and app-template exports carry query and chart configuration only. Reopening reacquires evidence for the saved choices. Provider expansion, calculated earnings surprises, valuation and the Company Research redesign are outside this delivery. Review the App against provider responses acquired through the same browser journey. A result fails review if the chosen issuer or query bounds cannot be traced to the displayed rows, figures or document states, or if the conclusion promises coverage beyond those results. Controlled cases exercise empty, clipped, partial and failed results. The real-provider journey establishes live acceptance for the recorded inputs and installed candidate.
