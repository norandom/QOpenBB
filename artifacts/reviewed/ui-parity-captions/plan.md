## Numeric run limits

Count every browser click, check/uncheck, fill, keypress and scroll as one UI interaction, including setup and menu opening/selection. The nine dark cases have a ceiling of 240 interactions and 96 state-changing field edits. The conditional tenth case allows at most 60 additional interactions and 12 edits; the entire run ceiling is 300 interactions and 108 edits. Record counters before each operation. Read-only evaluations and screenshots do not consume the interaction budget. A budget is a ceiling, not an action target; do not retry a failed operation automatically.

Use a 4-second action/locator deadline, 20-second navigation deadline, and 12-second settlement deadline per edit, including `waitForUpdate`, inactive animation and two equal geometry samples at least 100 ms apart. Each case has a 90-second deadline except the eight-role Weight matrix (240 seconds) and padding case (120 seconds). Cap the dark browser journey at 900 seconds, conditional light journey at 180 seconds and total browser time at 1080 seconds. Startup has a 180-second deadline; cleanup has 90 seconds and runs from `finally` even after any case, action or journey deadline. Apply an outer wall-clock deadline of 1350 seconds including startup/cleanup. The controller must yield progress at intervals below 60 seconds while supervising the background run.

Reaching any interaction, edit, time or projection bound stops new actions, records the exact limit and affected case as unresolved, retains completed records, and starts owned cleanup. A timeout or overflow is a failed capture, not a pass with omitted values. Do not expand budgets in response to failure; a revised run requires a separately recorded plan change. If cleanup itself exceeds its deadline, retain an unresolved cleanup receipt naming only owned resources and hand it to the controller.
