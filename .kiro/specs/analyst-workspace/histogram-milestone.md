## Acceptance contract

1. Use the first selected numeric field in selection order. Category selection and the existing category aggregation happen before binning; each resulting observation contributes one count. Keep finite zero and negative observations. Missing or invalid values contribute no count. Other selected numeric fields remain saved but do not create histogram series.
2. Preserve the original default, explicit-count and explicit-boundary binning rules described in [design](histogram-design.md). X retains the selected field's raw magnitude and unit; Y is dimensionless Frequency, never currency, sum or density. Keep empty intervals in the calculated domain without inventing zero-frequency observations.
3. Preserve reachable chart selection, category and aggregation, numeric X/Y axis position and styling, bin-count, tooltip, stroke width/dash/opacity, fill opacity, labels, shadow, title, legend, background, padding and theme controls. Keep their existing option paths and precedence. Histogram has no category/series inversion. No new editor, hidden activation flag or disabled placeholder satisfies acceptance.
4. Preserve navigator and mini-chart, numeric-window zoom/pan/reset, interval-intersection Y autoscale, refresh, save/reopen and export. Histogram does not inherit XY's four-observation minimum. Empty data and removing/re-adding the numeric selection must recover through the same session owner.
5. Prove displayed boundaries, frequencies and tooltips against the captured 40 AAPL quarterly FMP observations through the application. Select revenue explicitly, with None category and aggregation disabled, because the CSV also contains numeric fiscalYear. Record endpoint, parameters, retrieval time, capture hash, source and candidate-image identity without credentials. A provider entitlement failure remains a visible gap and cannot become a synthetic pass.
6. Complete independent source and application review, prior-family regressions, scope inventory, complexity checks and breadth/checklist reconciliation before closing the milestone. Publish one completed-feature alpha, then verify release and registry identities and anonymous pulls separately.

The equal-integer explicit-count repair and historical final-bin catch-all are explicit decisions in the design. They must appear in tests and evidence; neither supports an unqualified claim of exact parity. Standalone/AI callers, box plot, radial/specialty charts, general distribution analytics and currency-format improvements remain outside this slice.
